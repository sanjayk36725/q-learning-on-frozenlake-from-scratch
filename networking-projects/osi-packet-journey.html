At a glance

Encapsulation: each sender layer adds the control information it needs.

Decapsulation: the receiver examines and removes the relevant headers as data moves up the layers.

Important: the OSI model is a conceptual reference model. Real protocol stacks do not always map one-to-one to its seven layers.
